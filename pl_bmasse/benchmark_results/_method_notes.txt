Benchmark notes (log10-first pipeline)
=====================================
Source CSVs were NOT modified.
Train: unique planets with observed pl_bmasse, excluding validate planets.
Test: 389 planets in planet_true_validate.csv (originally missing mass).
All positive continuous features + target log10-transformed before modeling.
Domain M-R codes (Forecaster, Wolfgang, MRExo) receive physical radius/mass
because those published APIs apply their own internal log transforms.

Files:
- lalande2024estimating_knnxkde.xlsx      (GitHub knnxkde.py)
- lalande2024estimating_missforest.xlsx  (ExtraTrees IterativeImputer)
- lalande2024estimating_knnimputer.xlsx
- lalande2024estimating_mice.xlsx
- lalande2024estimating_gain.xlsx        (PyTorch port of their GAIN/)
- chen2017probabilistic.xlsx             (Forecaster Rstat2M)
- wolfgang2016probabilistic.xlsx         (postsamp_eqn2_baseline)
- kanodia2023beyond.xlsx                 (MRExo 2D M|R fit on our train set)
- ma2021predicting.xlsx                  (3-comp GMM M-R-F conditional mean;
                                          Zenodo MCMC archive is ~626MB and was
                                          not fully retrieved; flux proxy F=(Teq/278.33)^4)
- ulmer2019beyond.xlsx                   (BEM-inspired RF for mass; published BEM
                                          predicts radius, not mass)
- _benchmark_summary.xlsx

Each Excel has sheets: predictions (pl_name, predicted_mass, actual_mass), metrics.

- fbmi.xlsx  (GZanos/Fuzzy_Multiple_Imputation FBMI/FBLiR; log10 then inverse)
